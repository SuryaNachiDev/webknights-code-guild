What is WebKnights?

A unique, hands-on learning experience designed to ignite a passion for technology and creativity in middle school students.

🖥️ Our Mission

WebKnights pairs knowledgeable high school mentors with eager middle schoolers to teach them the fundamentals of web development. By the end of the program, every student will have created their own unique and interactive website.

Inclusivity: No previous knowledge required; everyone is welcome!

Interactive Learning: Students build their own websites from scratch!

Mentorship: Guidance from experienced high schoolers who share a love for coding.
